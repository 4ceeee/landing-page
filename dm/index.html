What worked06

Four things you can use this week.

You don’t need to hire anyone for these. They’re a few of the things I did at Vera-Fi Audio that you can try on your own.

Product photos

Shoot every product on the same background, at 1:1.

Shoppers compare products side by side. When every photo matches, the products look like one brand, and the store looks like a store you can trust.

Email

Email the people who already bought from you, by interest.

Past buyers and sign-ups are usually the easiest sales to win. Send each group the emails that match what they bought or asked about. At Vera-Fi Audio, campaigns averaged an 11.6% click rate.

Promotion

Use YouTubers for launches, not for steady sales.

Lead with your award winners the rest of the time. Reviews give a launch its spike, and the store and email have to carry the months in between.

Support

Write the manual so customers don’t have to email you.

A clear, designed manual answers the setup questions before they reach your inbox, and it makes the product feel finished.
