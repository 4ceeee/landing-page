Questions03

Fair questions.

Will a store take away the personal touch my customers like?

It didn’t at Vera-Fi Audio. Customers still talk to us personally through the store chat and email. Email campaigns are sent by interest, so people get the emails that match what they care about, not a blast to everyone.

You work with an audio brand. Is that a conflict?

I don’t work with direct competitors of my clients. If you make a different kind of equipment, serve a different market, or go for a different sound, it isn’t a conflict. Your numbers stay private unless you agree otherwise.

Why start with something so small?

Because hiring someone you’ve never worked with is a risk. A small fix lets you judge the work, the communication and the timing first. It’s how I started with Vera-Fi Audio: social graphics first, then the store, email and the rest.

Do you have time for another brand?

Yes. Vera-Fi Audio runs on systems that are already in place, so I have room for small fixes and one larger project at a time.

Will the changes break what already sells?

No. I add improvements one at a time, the way I did at Vera-Fi Audio. Nothing that sells today gets switched off.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

Small fixes are fixed-price, from $150, and most take about a week. Larger projects start at $1,200 and usually run up to two months. Either way: half up front, the rest once you’ve approved the work.

Do you work with brands outside the US?

Yes. I work remotely with US and Asian brands.
